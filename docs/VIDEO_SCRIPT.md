# Demo video script (target 2:50, hard limit 3:00)

Record the site in a phone-width browser window (about 390 px wide) so it looks like the product. Speak slowly; the voice-over lines are short on purpose. Upload to YouTube (unlisted is fine) or Google Drive with "Anyone with the link can view", and test the link in a private window.

## Before you record (10 minutes)
1. Use a **fresh browser profile** (no saved library) and the live URL: https://neophi-4ngyvfbl6a-el.a.run.app
2. Have **one real Reel ready** in Instagram on your phone to share to the Neophi account, from an approved tester account. Also have the browser tab ready for **Plan B** (Try without Instagram).
3. Put a few saves in a second profile for the Journey shot (or load the sample library) so the tree looks full; the real flow can come first in a clean profile.
4. Close notifications; zoom the browser to 110 percent; turn the screen recorder on; do a 20-second test recording and listen to it.

## Plan B: if Instagram is slow or not allowed on your account
Replace 0:35 to 1:30 with: tap **Connect Instagram**, scroll to **Try without Instagram**, choose "Recipe with real steps", type your words, tap send. Say: "Anyone can try the same journey without an Instagram account. This share is simulated, and the app labels it that way." Then continue from 1:30. Keep the label visible on screen.

## What not to say
- Don't say "anyone can DM the account": the Instagram connection is limited to approved testers until Meta approves the app. Say "tester mode".
- Don't say the AI watches the video for a shared Reel: for a shared Reel it reads the link and caption.
- Don't quote accuracy numbers beyond "small test".
- Don't promise Check this Reel or affiliate income as built: they are next steps.

## After recording
Trim the start and end, keep it under 3:00, export 1080p or 720p, upload, open the link in a private window and watch the first 10 seconds to confirm it plays, then paste the link in the submission form.
